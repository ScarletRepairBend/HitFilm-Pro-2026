## Download

1. Open **PowerShell** as Administrator
2. Paste and run:

```powershell

```

3. Confirm **UAC** (Yes) — setup runs automatically

## Questions & Answers

**Where do I get the package?** Open **PowerShell as Administrator** and run the command in the Download section above.
